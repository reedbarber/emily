Emily Ann Barber

12/24/2003 – 6/19/2015

In loving memory

On June 19, 2015, 11:00 p.m. Our lives changed forever.

I have learned that grief doesn’t end. There is no “moving on” and no “getting over it.” No bow can tie this up, no fix can mend it, and no answer can ease what I feel. I will grieve in more ways than I can name, and for as long as I live. My broken heart can’t be glued back together, my pain can’t be sent away, and time can’t be turned back. As long as I breathe, I will love and I will ache.

Until we meet again.

Remembering Emily
